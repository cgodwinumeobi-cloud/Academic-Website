# Uniform site-wide page banners

All page titles use the same navy-to-blue gradient (#101f35 to #254b68), a 150px minimum banner height, and centered titles/subtitles. The About Me custom banner is centered. The homepage welcome banner keeps its left greeting and right clock, with mobile stacking.

## Installation
Back up your existing project. Extract this folder and copy its contents into your project, preserving your latest working `index.qmd` and `images/` folder. The bundled `index.qmd` is an older homepage and may not contain your current live clock. Do not delete your old project before checking.

Preview with `quarto preview`. The actual photographs are not included in this archive.
