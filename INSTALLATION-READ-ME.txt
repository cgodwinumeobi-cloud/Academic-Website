GODWIN UMEOBI WEBSITE — CONSOLIDATED QUARTO SOURCE

This package merges Units 1–6 into one site folder.

IMPORTANT: This is NOT a verified replacement for every file in your live project.
The available original site archive contained no actual images, and its index.qmd
may be older than the homepage you currently use in Positron.

SAFEST INSTALLATION:
1. Make a full backup copy of your current website folder.
2. Open this extracted folder and your existing website folder side by side.
3. Copy all files and subfolders from this package into your existing site,
   allowing replacements for the files we updated.
4. BEFORE replacing index.qmd, compare it to your CURRENT working homepage.
   If your current homepage has the working live date/time and profile design,
   KEEP YOUR CURRENT index.qmd instead of this packaged older version.
5. KEEP your existing images/ directory and all its files. This package's
   images/ folder is empty because the source archive had no photo assets.
6. KEEP any other personal assets, downloads, custom scripts, and unshared blog
   posts in your current project. Do not delete the existing site folder.
7. The older publications.qmd is no longer linked in navigation; research.qmd
   consolidates its sections. You may archive publications.qmd after review.
8. Run `quarto preview` or `quarto render` from the website project folder.

This bundle contains the new navigation, combined CSS, Research, Services,
Teaching, About, Podcast, Events, and three blog landing pages.
Existing research blog post drafts from the original archive are included.
New Faith & Lifestyle and Leadership post folders are currently empty.

If you want a truly drop-in, delete-old-and-replace-new package, provide a ZIP
of your CURRENT entire working site (including index.qmd and images/), so those
assets can be preserved and the full site can be tested as a whole.
